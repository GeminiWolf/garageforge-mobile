## Main store listing

**App name** (30 max)

**Short description** (80 max)

**Full description** (4000 max)

Garage Forge keeps every car you're working on in one place: the parts it
needs, what they cost, and how far along the job is.

PARTS, FROM NEEDED TO FITTED
• Pick parts from the catalogue or type them in yourself
• Follow each part from To source → Sourcing → Awaiting delivery → Installed
• Mark urgent parts so they stand out
• Tick a part off when it's fitted, with undo if you tick the wrong one

KNOW WHAT IT COSTS
• Estimated and actual cost for every part, in rand, dollars or euros
• See what's been spent or ordered and what's still to buy, per car
• Set a monthly cap and get warned before you go over it

EVERYTHING ABOUT THE CAR
• Mileage, with the date you last read it
• Engine details from the catalogue, plus the car's engine number
• Log the time you spend working on each car
• Notes for what the customer reported, or reminders for yourself

YOUR GARAGE, BACKED UP
• Works without an account: everything stays on your phone
• Create a free account to back up your garage and use it on the Garage
  Forge website, on a bigger screen
• Changes sync between your phone and the website

SHARE IT
• Export a printable PDF summary of a car and its parts

No ads. No tracking.

## Data safety form

Overall:
- **Does the app collect or share user data?** Yes, it collects (does not share).
- **Is all data encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS only).
- **Can users request deletion?** Yes: in the app (Settings → Account →
  Delete account) and on the website (Account → Delete account).
- **Is data collection optional?** Yes for everything below: it's only
  collected when the user creates an account. Without one, data stays on the
  phone and is not collected.

Data types to declare (Collected = Yes, Shared = No, Processed ephemerally = No,
Optional = Yes):

| Play category | Data type | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info | Name | App functionality, Account management | Shown on the account |
| Personal info | Email address | App functionality, Account management | Sign-in and account emails |
| Personal info | Other info | App functionality | Vehicle registration number, VIN and engine number, if entered |
| Financial info | Other financial info | App functionality | Part costs and payment dates the user types in (no card or bank data) |
| App activity | Other user-generated content | App functionality | Vehicles, parts, mileage, time logged and notes |

Do **not** declare: location, contacts, photos, messages, health, web
history, device or other IDs, analytics, crash logs or diagnostics. The app
has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. If one is added later (for example
Sentry), add "Crash logs" and "Diagnostics" under App info and performance.

**Catalogue lookups** (makes, models, parts) go to our server without any
account or personal data, so they are not user data.

## Closed testing (before production)

1. Play Console → Testing → Closed testing → create a track (e.g. "Beta").
2. Upload `app-release.aab` (version 2.1.0, code 10).
3. Add testers by email list or Google Group. If your developer account is a
   personal one created after November 2023, Google requires at least 12
   testers opted in for 14 days in a row before you can apply for production.
   Play Console's dashboard shows the exact requirement for your account.
4. Send testers the opt-in link from the track page.
